About Us

Who we are

CiKiMi Game Studio was founded on a simple belief: games should be fun, friendly, and for everyone. We make light, colorful games that are easy to pick up and satisfying to play, no matter how much time you have.

Our small team focuses on bright designs, simple mechanics, and the little moments of joy that make casual gaming special. We craft every game with care, because we play games too.

Our Mission To create bright, cheerful games that make everyday moments more fun.
Our Approach Simple ideas, careful polish, and games that respect the player's time.
Our Audience Casual players of all ages who enjoy quick, relaxing, colorful play.
